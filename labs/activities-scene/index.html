Rebuild the scene

Version 2. A guide takes you through a museum scene one step at a time. Each step tells you a little, then hands you one person or thing. You reason from the clue: where does it go, and what does the person do? Then the step shows what the museum source says. Draft · research preview

My recommendation. Build the step engine as one new, additive activity kind (place), and ship two scenes with strong sources: C · Inside the chise (things) and A · Survey camp (people). Herring and mine come after we find a second source for their tasks. Voice and captions are on in every scene.

What changed from version 1

  1. No "read, cover, recall". That felt like a memory game. Now you learn in small steps while you build.
  2. A clue to reason from. Each step asks a question you can work out ("smoke makes fish keep: where do they hang?"). The source then confirms it.
  3. One figure per step. You never pick from a row of the same "Worker". The step hands you one person, named by their own hat and colour.
  4. Only real choices. Two or three rings, and four poses on the sheet. Wrong answers get a hint; after two tries, "Show me".
  5. The scene grows. At the end you see the full scene and a recap of every cited point, like the museum label.
  6. Same rules as before. No posed Ainu figures in Ainu scenes; histories kept apart; "seen in the diorama" marked as a weaker source.
