# Rights for this folder

The files in this folder are transcriptions and draft translations of
exhibition panels, labels and Q&A cards at the Hokkaido Museum
(北海道博物館, Sapporo). The original text, images and design belong to the
Hokkaido Museum and to the people and institutions it credits.

- This folder is **not** under the repository's MIT license. The MIT license
  covers original code and original placeholder geometry only.
- Purpose: research notes for a non-commercial hackathon prototype that is
  partnered with the Government of Hokkaido (decision Lukas, 2026-11-25). The
  museum has not given written permission.
- Do not ship this text verbatim in the app. The app uses short cited
  paraphrases (claims with source ids and locators, see docs/REVIEW.md).
- Names of makers, tellers and authors are as printed on the panels. Credit
  them with any derived content.
- Withdrawal: if the museum or a credited person asks, remove the affected
  text from this folder and from every claim that cites it.
- The translations marked "EN (mine)" are drafts by Claude and are not
  reviewed.
