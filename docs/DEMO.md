## Story-first submitted video outline

Working outline for reported 5–6 minutes, pending written reconciliation with the deck’s older ≤3-minute limit.

0:00–0:40: Why cultural knowledge needs both careful preservation and engaging discovery. Show the people/workflow the product serves without invented testimonials.
0:40–1:40: Contributor supplies a harmless source; extraction carries its evidence; a human edits and approves. Identify mocks honestly.
1:40–3:40: Follow the owl into one room, inspect a source-linked object, hear narration and complete a reflection.
3:40–4:30: Open the Heritage Journal; show the approved contribution in the same experience and the ability to withdraw it.
4:30–5:30: Hokkaido usefulness, intended community collaboration and what is reusable. Finish with the coherent product vision.

Three-minute contingency: 25s problem, 45s capture/review, 80s visitor/journal, 30s value/reusability. Never describe a research preview as a community-approved collection.

Include model training only if actually done. Four scoring dimensions get concrete evidence, not technology name-dropping.

Submission checklist: correct public repository, root MIT LICENSE, README reproducible run, no secrets/personal data/unauthorized assets; verify categories and sponsor tags before Nov 26 14:00 JST; video/writeup/deck/pitch and Submitted status by 14:30 JST.
