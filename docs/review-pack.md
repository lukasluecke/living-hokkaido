## How to use this pack

1. Work place by place. The places with the most Ainu-language and sacred items come first.
2. In each place, the highest-risk items come first.
3. For each item, mark **Approve** or **Fix** and write the fix in the last column. A fix can also be "remove".
4. Give the marked pack back to the team. The team then sets the data to the reviewed state with your name and date, and removes the row from `docs/REVIEW.md`. The team never adds a reviewer name for you.

An item can carry more than one risk. It is sorted by its highest risk. "Ask the owl" can answer from every claim of a place, so each claim can also show there.

**Decision key:** write `A` (approve) or `F` (fix) in the Decision column.

## Start here: eight questions that decide many rows

Each answer settles a group of rows in the place tables. Answer these first.

1. **Ainu words.** Which spelling for `attus` (attus / attush / ahrus), and is `isitaykini` the right word for this loom (Wikipedia names the backstrap loom attushi karape)? Rows: `term:attus`, `term:isitaykini`, `term-attus`, `term-isitaykini`.
2. **Our Latin spellings.** The museum prints greetings, cise words and sap/sat/sak in katakana. The Latin spellings are ours. Are they correct for the region? Rows: `romanised-by-us`, `greet-*`, `cise-plan`, `sap-sat-sak`.
3. **The upper seat.** May the upper seat, its treasures and the window of the kamuy seat be shown? Today the 3D house shows the upper seat but not the window, and the cise map shows the window. Rows: `panel-treasures`, `cise-plan`, `visual:house-interior`, `visual:map-cise-plan`, `visual:place-cise`.
4. **Ritual and oral literature.** Sakehe, the owl story, kamuy and the canoe as a kamuy are included in full (decision Lukas). Keep, change or remove? Rows: `sakehe`, `owl-sakehe`, `owl-kamuy`, `kamuy-*`, `canoe-kamuy`, `activity:story-owl`.
5. **Named people.** Do the families and rights holders agree to the names and credit? Fujitani Rumiko, Suteno Orita, Ume Nishihira and Tomita Tomoko, Kura Sunazawa, Takeichi Moritake, Yaichiro Hama, Nabe Shirasawa, Kayano Shigeru.
6. **Machine translation of Ainu.** The app no longer blocks machine translation of Ainu in curator memories. It shows a "Draft" label. Restore the block? Row: `gate:ainu-translation`.
7. **Diorama-only facts.** Four scene steps and two claims rest only on what museum figures show. Keep them with the "weaker source" label, or remove them? Rows: `sea-diorama-tasks`, `mine-diorama-tasks`, `place-herring-yard:*`, `place-mine-forest:*`.
8. **Panel text rights.** All museum sources have rights "unknown" and consent "pending". Who asks the Hokkaido Museum, and may the paraphrases ship before the answer?

An ID with a prefix (`term:`, `activity:`, `era:`, `visual:`, `audio:`, `voice:`, `memory:`, `gate:`) is not a claim. An ID with no prefix is a claim in `src/content/bark-to-cloth` (or `vendor/out-of-place` for the Otaru and Sapporo games). A row with a `place-…:step` ID is one step of a Build the scene activity.
