# Notice: what the MIT license covers

The MIT license in [LICENSE](LICENSE) covers only the original work in this
repository: source code, prompts, schemas, the training pipeline, and the
character sheets written for this project.

It does **not** cover:

- The Omusha diorama in the Hokkaido Museum (北海道博物館), or any photograph,
  video, Gaussian splat or other reproduction of it. These are not in this
  repository. The `assets/` folder is excluded from version control, and the
  app loads the museum capture at runtime only where permission has been granted.
- Quoted source material in the retrieval corpus. Each excerpt keeps its own
  attribution and is included only as a short quotation.

To run the app without museum material, use your own capture (see
[sample/README.md](sample/README.md)).

## About the voices

The characters are synthetic interpretations built from historical sources.
They are not historical records and do not speak for any person or community.
Ainu characters in particular give sourced, limited answers and decline to
invent Ainu belief or opinion.
